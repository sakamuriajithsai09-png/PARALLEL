Problem → Answer

Why this project?

✕ The Problem

  • • Students may not know where to report campus issues.
  • • Complaints can be delayed, misplaced, or hard to track.
  • • Manual routing to the correct department takes time.
  • • Students receive little visibility after submitting.

✓ Our Answer

  • • One digital platform for complaints across campus.
  • • AI classifies the issue, priority, and department.
  • • Admin assigns staff and tracks resolution status.
  • • Notifications, feedback, and analytics close the loop.

A transparent complaint-to-resolution cycle

Smart Campus Workflow

13 steps — from opening the app to AI analytics.

01

Open the App

Login with student credentials or register as a new user. A simple entry point for all campus issues.

Goal: easy to start
02

Student Dashboard

Welcome message, profile access, existing complaints and notifications — with a "New Complaint" button front and center.

Goal: everything in one place
03

Select Complaint Type

Choose the campus area: College, Hostel, Mess, Transport, Wi-Fi, Electricity, Water — or Cleaning, Maintenance, Security, Other. E.g. "Mess".

Narrows to the right service area
04

Select the Problem

Issue types relevant to the category appear: Food Quality, Quantity, Hygiene, Taste, Menu, Timing, Other. E.g. "Food Quality" selected.

Less typing • clearer classification
05

Describe the Problem

"The food served today was not properly cooked." Upload a photo 📷, pick a location 📍 (e.g. Hostel Mess 1), and submit. Good details help staff solve it.

Evidence + context
06

AI Checks the Complaint

08

Admin Dashboard

The manager receives CMP1025 — Food Quality • Hostel Mess 1 • Priority: Medium — and can Accept → Assign Staff → Start Work.

Right team, clear task, full context
09

Staff Resolves It

Assigned staff investigates the actual problem, work begins, and progress is recorded. Clear ownership reduces delays and missed complaints.

Ownership = speed
10

Track Resolution Status

1 · PENDING→ 2 · IN PROGRESS→ 3 · RESOLVED

A visible lifecycle — students and admins always know where a complaint stands.

11

Student Notification 🔔

"Complaint CMP1025 has been resolved."

No repeated trips to the office — the loop closes with timely communication.

12

Student Feedback

Was your problem solved? Yes ✓ / No ✕ plus a star rating ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐. Feedback evaluates service quality and feeds future improvement.
